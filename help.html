Help & Documentation

Get help with using the Supply Chain & Inventory Management System.

Getting Started

1. Login to the System

Use your email and password to sign in, then enter the 6-digit verification code sent to your email.

2. Navigate Using the Sidebar

Click any module in the left sidebar. Use the toggle button to collapse or expand it.

3. Quick QR Scan (from any page)

Tap the floating scan button at the bottom-right corner of any screen to scan a QR code — pick the action (intake, check-out, assign, move) and point the camera.

4. Access Your Profile

Click your profile picture in the top-right corner for profile settings, security options, and log out.

Common Tasks

How do I scan a QR code?

Click the floating scan button (bottom-right) on any page, choose the action, and point your camera — or go to Smart Warehousing for zone-aware scanning.

What happens when an item is checked out?

The asset is marked Deployed, an immutable scan log entry is recorded, and if the category drops below its minimum stock level, a purchase requisition is created automatically.

How do I create a Purchase Order?

Go to Purchase Orders, click Create PO, fill in supplier and items, then submit for approval.

How do I change my profile photo?

Go to My Profile and click the camera icon on your avatar. Name, email and role are locked — contact your administrator to change them.
