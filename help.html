Help & Documentation

Get help with using the Supply Chain & Inventory Management System

Getting Started

1. Login to the System

Use your email and password to login. If MFA is enabled, you'll need to enter the OTP sent to your email.

2. Navigate Using the Sidebar

Click on any module in the left sidebar to access different features. Use the toggle button to collapse/expand the sidebar.

3. Access Your Profile

Click on your profile picture in the top-right corner to access profile settings, security options, and logout.

Module Documentation

warehouse Smart Warehousing

Manage warehouse zones, scan QR codes for assets, and track warehouse operations.

Features: • Create and manage warehouse zones • Scan QR codes to track assets • View recent scan activity • Filter scans by type (check-in, check-out, transfer)

inventory_2 Inventory Management

Track and manage all inventory assets, locations, and transactions.

Features: • Add, edit, and delete inventory assets • Track asset locations and zones • View asset history and transactions • QR code management for each asset

shopping_cart Procurement & Sourcing

Manage requisitions, supplier quotes, and procurement processes.

Features: • Create and approve requisitions • Compare supplier quotes • Track procurement pipeline • Manage sourcing activities

groups Supplier Management

Manage supplier relationships, contacts, and performance.

Features: • Add and manage supplier records • Track supplier performance metrics • Store contact information • View supplier history

receipt_long Purchase Orders

Create, approve, and track purchase orders throughout their lifecycle.

Features: • Create and submit purchase orders • Approve or reject POs • Track PO status and shipping • Receive goods and generate QR codes • View complete PO lifecycle

description Documents & Logistics

Manage shipping documents, receipts, and logistics documentation.

Features: • Upload and manage shipping documents • Track document status • Digital signatures for documents • Courier receipt management

Common Tasks

How to scan a QR code?

1. Go to Smart Warehousing module

2. Click "Enable Camera" to activate your device camera

3. Point the camera at a QR code - it will be automatically detected and recorded

How to create a Purchase Order?

1. Go to Purchase Orders module

2. Click "Create PO" button

3. Fill in supplier, items, and quantities

4. Submit for approval

How to add a new asset?

1. Go to Inventory Management module

2. Click "Add Asset" button

3. Fill in asset details including QR code, location, and zone

How to enable MFA?

1. Go to Security & MFA Settings

2. Click "Enable MFA"

3. Scan the QR code with your authenticator app or enter the secret key
